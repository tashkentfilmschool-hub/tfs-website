01 / The schoolTashkent · Uzbekistan

Local stories.
Global language.

Tashkent Film School is a progressive, independent school of film and visual practices in Uzbekistan.

Founded in 2022, TFS gives emerging filmmakers from Uzbekistan and across Central Asia access to professional tools, working mentors and a community in which their own stories can take form.

TFS works with aspiring and established filmmakers, artists and interdisciplinary creators from Uzbekistan and beyond through courses, screenings, artist talks and public programming.
