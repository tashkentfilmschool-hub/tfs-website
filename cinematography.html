Course / Cinematography

See the frame.
Shape the story.

A two-month course in camera work, light, composition and documentary storytelling with Umida Ahmedova.

Course overview

The TFS source materials describe this course as a two-month introduction to the fundamentals of camera work, light, composition and documentary storytelling. It combines technical training with an artistic, human-centered approach.

Participants learn how to see the frame, develop a visual style and build a strong foundation in cinematography through theory and hands-on sessions.

What the course focuses on

  • Camera work and technical foundations
  • Light and composition
  • Documentary storytelling
  • Developing an individual visual language
  • Practice alongside theory

Mentor

Umida Ahmedova is presented in the TFS materials as a documentary filmmaker, cinematographer and photographer, and as the first woman cinematographer in Central Asia.
